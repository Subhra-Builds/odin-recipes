FriedRice

Description

Fried rice is a classic, quick-cooking dish made by stir-frying cooked rice over high heat with a mix of crisp vegetables, aromatics like garlic and spring onions, and savoury sauces. The secret to getting that signature smoky, non-mushy texture is using day-old, chilled rice—the grains dry out slightly in the fridge, allowing them to separate easily and soak up all the flavour in the pan without turning sticky.

One of the best things about fried rice is how endlessly adaptable it is. While a basic version relies on soy sauce, a dash of vinegar, and black pepper for its punchy umami taste, you can easily customize it with scrambled eggs, diced chicken, prawns, or tofu. Ready in under 15 minutes, it is the ultimate comfort meal for turning leftover ingredients into something vibrant and deeply satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked, chilled day-old rice (basmati or jasmine)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable or sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1/2 cup spring onions, chopped (separate the white and green parts)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables, finely diced (carrots, french beans, and capsicum)
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten (optional)
  • 1.5 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar or rice vinegar
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Prep the rice: Gently break up any large clumps in the chilled rice with your fingers or a fork so the grains separate easily in the pan.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat the oil in a large wok or wide pan over high heat. Add the minced garlic and the white parts of the spring onions, stir-frying for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Cook the vegetables: Toss in the diced carrots, beans, and capsicum. Stir-fry on high heat for 2 to 3 minutes so they cook through while keeping a slight crunch.
  4. Scramble the eggs (if using): Push the veggies to one side of the pan, pour the beaten eggs into the empty space, and scramble them until just set before mixing them with the vegetables.
  5. Toss the rice and sauces: Add the rice to the pan along with the soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and a pinch of salt. Toss everything vigorously over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes until the rice is piping hot and evenly coated.
  6. Garnish and serve: Turn off the heat, stir in the green parts of the spring onions, and serve immediately.
